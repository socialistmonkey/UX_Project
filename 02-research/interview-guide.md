# Step 2 · Interview guide

I will run the interviews online. I post a short call for participants in a glitch art community (for example a subreddit), and people answer in writing (as a reply or a private message) or in a short chat. The ten questions are the same for everyone. I never mention my app idea. I ask about what people actually did, not what they would do, and I follow up with "why?" and "what happened then?". Questions 3 to 7 are the core.

Task: making glitch art on purpose and saving the result so the file still opens.

1. How would you describe what you do with glitch art or databending, and how long have you been doing it?
2. What do you usually glitch (photos, scans, video, something else), and how often?
3. Think about the last piece you made. Describe the steps you took from the beginning, in your own words.
4. Which tools or programs did you use for it, and why those?
5. What was the first thing that went wrong or surprised you?
6. Did a file ever break, fail to open or get lost? What happened?
7. What did you do about it?
8. If you could change one thing about how you work now, what would it be?
9. Was there something you expected to be able to do but could not?
10. Is there anything I did not ask that matters? And may I contact you again later for a short test?

## Post for the community
> I am a design student doing research for a university course on how people make glitch art and databend files. I am not selling anything. If you have made glitch work, I would love to hear about your process. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes, and you can answer by message or in the comments, whatever you prefer. I will not use your name or username anywhere, only a code like P1. Message me if you would like to take part and I will send the questions.

## Rules I follow
- Before posting, I read the community's rules on surveys and self-promotion. If posting is not allowed, I ask a moderator first.
- I send the questions by private message, and the consent text above comes first.
- I do not save usernames or links. Each person's answers go into a file with a code (P1, P2) and I paraphrase or quote only what they wrote about their work.
- People can skip any question or stop at any time, and I say so before I start.
- If someone is happy to do it, I also ask them to share their screen for five minutes while they use one glitch tool, so I have one observation of a real session.
