# Step 1 · Idea

## Working title
Image Bender

## The idea in one sentence
Image Bender lets glitch artists corrupt an image byte by byte and lock safe zones so the file still opens, for when preset filters feel fake and databending by hand keeps destroying the file.

## Who it is for
People who make glitch art on purpose, and people who just love the look of corruption and would use a tool like this.

## Problem hypothesis (to be tested in Steps 2 and 3, not a fact yet)
Glitch artists who databend by hand, for example in a text editor or an audio editor, often break the file and cannot predict the result. Filter apps are safe, but they feel like presets and not like real corruption. I believe these people want real corruption of the actual file data, with control over which parts must never break.

## Platform and why
macOS desktop (Electron app). Image Bender already exists as a Mac app, and the people who would use it work on images at a computer.

## Main task (one sentence)
Corrupt one image and export it so that it still opens.

## Three things the app will not do
1. No AI. There are no AI filters and no AI generation, and every effect is a mechanical corruption of the data.
2. No video, music or animation. The video, datamosh, music-reactive and frame-by-frame animation features are out of scope for this course project, and I removed them from the project copy of the app.
3. No general photo editing. There is no crop, colour correction or retouching.
