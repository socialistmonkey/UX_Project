# Step 1 · Idea

## Working title
Image Bender

## The idea in one sentence
Image Bender helps glitch artists to corrupt an image byte by byte, with locked safe zones that keep the file openable, when preset filters feel fake and hand-databending keeps destroying their files.

## Who it is for, and where I will find five of them
People who make glitch or datamosh art on purpose, and people who love the corruption aesthetic and would use a tool like this.

## Problem hypothesis (to be tested in Steps 2 and 3, not a fact yet)
Glitch artists who databend by hand (text editor, audio editor) often break the file and cannot predict the result, while filter apps stay safe but feel like presets rather than real corruption. I believe they want real, mechanical corruption of the actual file data with control over which parts must never break.

## Platform and why
macOS desktop (Electron app). The tool already exists as a Mac app, and its users work on images at a computer.
Open point: the course brief lists iOS, Android and responsive web, so desktop needs Dr. Balogh's approval.
Fallback if he says no: an iPhone version of the same journey (corrupt one photo, lock a safe zone, export).

## Main task (one sentence)
Corrupt one image and export it so that it still opens.

## Three things the app will not do
1. No AI: no AI filters, no AI generation. Every effect is a mechanical corruption of the data.
2. No video in this project: the video and datamosh category is out of scope for the course version.
3. No general photo editing: no crop, colour correction or retouching.
